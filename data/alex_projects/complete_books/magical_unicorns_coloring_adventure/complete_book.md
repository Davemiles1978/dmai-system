## Introduction

![Introduction](data/alex_projects/complete_books/graphics/intro_magical_unicorns_and_sparkles.svg)

Dear Children 4-8,

Welcome to this magical coloring adventure! Inside this book, you'll find magical unicorns and sparkles waiting for your creative touch.

**How to Use This Book:**
• Use crayons, colored pencils, or markers
• Stay inside the lines for best results
• Be creative - there's no wrong way to color!
• Each page is single-sided so you can remove and display your art

**Tips for Best Results:**
1. Start with lighter colors and add darker ones
2. Take breaks to rest your hand
3. Have fun and be creative!

Let your imagination soar!

**Happy Coloring!**

Alex Riviera
