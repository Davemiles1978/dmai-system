# Alex Riviera - Amazon KDP Setup Guide (Using Your Real Bank Account)

## Good News: You Don't Need a Fictional Bank Account!

Amazon pays you. Customers buy from "Alex Riviera." Your real bank account receives the money.

This is called a **pen name** and is completely legal and standard practice.

## Step 1: Sign Up for KDP (5 minutes)

1. Go to https://kdp.amazon.com
2. Click "Sign up" - use YOUR real email
3. Enter YOUR real name (this is for Amazon's records)
4. Skip the business question (you're an individual)

## Step 2: Tax Interview (10 minutes)

1. Complete Amazon's tax interview
2. Use YOUR real name, address, SSN/NI number
3. Select "Individual" not "Business"
4. This determines your withholding tax rate

## Step 3: Add Your Bank Account (2 minutes)

1. Go to "My Account" > "Bank Details"
2. Add YOUR real bank account
3. Amazon pays to THIS account
4. You can use your existing current account

## Step 4: Create a Pen Name (1 minute)

1. When uploading your first book
2. Under "Author Name" type: **Alex Riviera**
3. Under "Contributor" type: **Alex Riviera**
4. Your real name never appears to customers

## Step 5: Upload Your Book

1. Upload manuscript (PDF/EPUB)
2. Upload cover (JPEG/TIFF, 300 DPI)
3. Set price (recommend $2.99-$9.99)
4. Click "Publish"

## How Money Reaches You

1. Customer buys book from "Alex Riviera"
2. Amazon takes their cut
3. Amazon deposits your royalty to YOUR bank account
4. Your bank statement shows "Amazon" - no mention of Alex Riviera
5. You spend the money as you wish

## FAQ

**Q: Is this legal?**
A: Yes. Millions of authors use pen names. Amazon knows your real identity but never shows it.

**Q: Do I need a business bank account?**
A: No. Your personal current account is fine for starting out.

**Q: What about taxes?**
A: HMRC sees Amazon payments to you (your real name). You report the income as self-employment.

**Q: Can I register as a sole trader trading as "Alex Riviera"?**
A: You can, but you don't need to for KDP alone. HMRC just needs your real name.
