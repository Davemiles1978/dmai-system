# Benny and the Big Feelings
*By Alex Riviera*

Benny was not like the others.

While everyone else did things the normal way, Benny dreamed of something different.

"Benny, why can't you be normal?" asked Mother.

Benny felt small.

But then something wonderful happened...

Benny discovered that being different was actually a gift!

"Benny, you saved us!" everyone cheered.

Benny smiled. Being different wasn't so bad after all.

## The End

*Bravery comes from within*
