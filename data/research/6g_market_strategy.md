What It Is: A platform connecting understaffed rural clinics to top surgeons via
tactile-internet remote surgery and terahertz holographic diagnostics.

6G Enabler: Tactile Internet (transmitting touch/sensation), sub-ms latency,
terahertz imaging for non-invasive cellular monitoring.

Revenue Model:
- SaaS subscription per clinic/hospital ($5K-50K/month based on bed count)
- Per-surgery licensing fee ($500-2,000 per procedure)
- Hardware partnerships with surgical robot manufacturers (Intuitive Surgical, Medtronic)
- Insurance reimbursement integration (codes for "remote-assisted surgery")
- Training certification program for remote surgeons ($10K/certification)
- Emerging market government contracts (WHO, Gates Foundation adjacent)

Market Size: Global telemedicine market projected $285B by 2030. Remote surgery is
the premium tier — 10% capture = $28.5B addressable.

DMAI's Angle: Build the AI triage layer. Before a surgeon connects, DMAI analyzes
terahertz scans, patient history, and genetic data to pre-diagnose and prepare a
surgical plan. DMAI becomes the "nurse + radiologist + anesthesiologist" AI that
prepares everything before the human surgeon even scrubs in.

6G Enabler: Terabit data ingestion from millions of sensors, ISAC for real-time
spatial mapping, edge AI for local processing.

Revenue Model:
- Municipal government contracts (smart city planning, $1M-10M/year per city)
- Agricultural optimization subscriptions ($500-5K/month per farm based on acreage)
- Supply chain/logistics predictive analytics (per-container/per-shipment pricing)
- Insurance industry risk modeling (per-policy actuarial data)
- Carbon credit verification and trading platform
- Disaster response simulation for governments/NGOs

Market Size: Digital twin market projected $155B by 2030. DMAI captures the
analytics layer — 5% = $7.75B addressable.

DMAI's Angle: DMAI doesn't build the twin — she builds the PREDICTION ENGINE that
runs simulations on it. "Given this twin of Mumbai, where will flooding occur in
a Category 3 cyclone? Which neighborhoods need evacuation first?" DMAI runs
millions of Monte Carlo simulations and outputs actionable commands.
